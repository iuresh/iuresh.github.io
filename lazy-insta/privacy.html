Lazy Insta – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Lazy Insta is a browser extension that adds keyboard shortcuts and time statistics to Instagram. We built it so that your data stays on your computer. We do not run servers that receive your data, and we do not collect, sell or share anything about you.

What the extension stores

All of the following is saved only in your browser's extension storage:

  • Your settings: the usernames and keyboard shortcuts you add, playback speed, rewind time, undo time and theme. If Chrome sync is turned on, Chrome syncs these settings between your own browsers through your Google account.
  • Usage statistics: when your Instagram sessions started and ended, how long you were active, how many reels you viewed per day, and which of your added users you shared each reel with. This history is deleted automatically after 90 days, or straight away when you click "Clear history".
  • Accounts you follow: to suggest usernames when you add someone, the extension reads the list of accounts you follow from Instagram while you are logged in, and keeps only their usernames and display names. This is refreshed at most once a day.

What is sent anywhere

Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. The extension talks only to instagram.com, in your own logged-in browser session, to do what you ask: open the share window, share a reel with the person you chose, and read the accounts you follow.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect personal information, browsing history, messages or passwords.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not sell or transfer your data.

Removing your data

Click "Clear history" on the Stats tab, or uninstall the extension. Uninstalling removes everything the extension has stored.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if we add optional paid features or cloud sync, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

Contact

Lazy Insta is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Instagram or Meta.
